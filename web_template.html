CCAR-F Study Deck

Spaced repetition over the atomic layer of the exam: mechanisms, trigger conditions, and the reason each anti-pattern fails.

  • One card, one fact. Every card answers in under ten seconds. A card holding four facts cannot be scheduled.
  • No multiple choice. Recognising an option is a weaker task than producing the answer. The mock exam simulator carries the scenario judgment; this deck carries the layer underneath it.
  • Answer honestly. Pressing Good on a card you half-knew is the fastest way to make the schedule useless.
  • Daily, in two sittings. Clear reviews first, then take the day's new cards. Twenty to thirty minutes covers a four-week run; a fortnight before the exam it is closer to forty, because the whole deck has to fit into half the time.
